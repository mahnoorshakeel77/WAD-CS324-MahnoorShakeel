Follow our Lead!

  1. Letting MiB analyze your face using your photos

    You have the option to take photos directly on our platform or you can upload your selfies from your device. Please ensure that you only attach image files, not videos, PDFs or documents. We recommend uploading a minimum of 3 selfies for the best results.

  2. Choosing your preferred look

    MiB can create a variety of looks for you, from everyday to special occasions. You can choose the closest option from our pre-defined list so that our AI gives you the most relevant recommendations for your desired look.

  3. Telling us how good you are at makeup

    MiB provides you with step-by-step instructions for your makeup look based on your skill level. The level of detail provided for each look is decided based on your comfort level. For example, if you're experienced with makeup but haven't ever attempted an editorial look, we recommend that you select the "Beginner" level for the most detailed instructions. If you are a professional MUA doing a festive look, you can select the "Advanced" level for a more concise overview of the look.

  4. Choosing your skin type

    MiB incorporates your skin type information in its analysis to recommend you products and techniques that best support a skin-like, airbrushed makeup finish on your face. The longevity of your makeup look can also be increased by the techniques and products our AI suggests, so if your skin type tends to fluctuate based on seasonal weather, or is currently sensitive due to the usage of certain skincare products, we recommend choosing your skin type accordingly. This is why we support the selection of multiple options for this.

  5. Writing about your additional preferences

    This is a space for you to describe extra preferences for your makeup look. If you prefer a dewy finish, tell us that. If you gravitate towards bolder lip colours or have a bright spring colour palette, let us know. If you are allergic to certain ingredients, please specify. Please do not use this space to ask questions or chat with the AI, this is only for you to tell us what you expect from MiB's analysis.

  6. Clicking "Analyze Muse"

    Once you have completed the previous steps and provided us with all there is to know about yourself, this button will initiate the analysis of your selfies and provide you with 3-5 looks that best match your expectations. You can select any one and display its execution plan. Then grab a seat, look in the mirror, and working that beauty blender!

Tips

Selfie tips for the best analysis

  • Start with a clean, dry, and moisturized face
  • Stand near a window with lots of natural daylight
  • Face the camera straight on, ensuring even lighting and no shadows
  • Skip any heavy makeup or skin-smoothing beauty filters
  • Take at least 3 selfies from different angles, including front, left, and right
  • Keep a relaxed, neutral expression
