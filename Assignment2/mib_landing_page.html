Our Story

Who We Are

We are and Mahnoor Shakeel and Hibba Hussain, the two people behind Muse in Beauty. MiB started from a simple idea: makeup should feel like creative play, not guesswork. We wanted to build a place where anyone, whether they have never held a blending brush or already know their way around a palette, can sit down in front of the mirror and know exactly what to do next.

Why We Built MiB

Too often, people find a look they adore on social media, but it comes with no tutorial. Others have an event coming up and the perfect outfit in mind, but no idea which makeup look will complement it. Beginners struggle to know which products and techniques create a certain look, and even busy students and working professionals find it hard to perfect a daily routine that looks put-together instead of half-asleep. Regular AI tools can help a little, but they are not trained well enough on makeup techniques or the huge range of products out there.

Our Vision

We imagine MiB as the quintessential muse for every makeup lover: a personalized coach that considers your facial features, the products you already own, and the events you are attending. It breaks every look down into step-by-step plans with the exact products, brushes, shades, and techniques you need. We are building it for beginners, creative artists recreating complex looks, people dressing for an occasion, and especially for those with deeper skin tones, such as South Asians and Africans, who often struggle to find the right shades. Because makeup should be simple and fun for everyone.

AI Notice

How does MiB use AI?

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence, which refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines. Muse in Beauty is powered by AI which is extensively trained on the vast amount of makeup data available online. Our makeup coach analyzes the photos and details you share, such as selfies, inspiration looks, outfits, and preferences, to suggest shades, products, brushes, and step-by-step techniques tailored to you. All of the guidance you see on MiB is generated by AI and not by a human makeup artist.

What are the limitations of MiB's AI?

Our makeup coach's AI-powered recommendations are suggestions, not guarantees of perfect results. Results can vary with lighting, photo quality, screen colour settings, and the products you use, so a suggested shade may look different in person. We encourage you to treat every suggestion as a starting point and to patch-test new products, especially if you have sensitive skin or allergies. MiB cannot provide medical or dermatological advice. Your feedback is being used constantly to improve the accuracy of our AI model.

Is MiB's AI dangerous?

MiB's AI has no potential to cause any harm. It is designed to provide helpful suggestions and recommendations solely for makeup application. However, as with any technology, it's important to use it responsibly and consider the limitations and potential variations in results. For further information on how the AI stores, uses, and understands your data, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
